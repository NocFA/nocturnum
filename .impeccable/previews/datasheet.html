General Description

notes-768 holds 48,210 points, each carrying one dense float32 vector named text of 768 dimensions compared by cosine distance. Two segments are sealed behind an HNSW graph with scalar int8 quantization and rescoring, while the third still takes writes and sits at 61% of its indexing threshold.

Features

  • 48,210 points in 3 segments, 2 indexed and 1 appendable
  • One dense vector text, 768 dims, cosine, float32
  • HNSW graph with m = 16, ef_construct = 100 and search ef = 128
  • Scalar int8 quantization, rescore on, oversampling 2.0
  • Payload indexes on source, tags, created_at and pinned
  • Recall@10 of 0.987 against exact search at ef = 128
  • Search latency 3.1 ms p50 and 9.8 ms p99 at ef = 128
  • Nightly snapshot at 03:00 with 7 kept, last one 2.9 GB
